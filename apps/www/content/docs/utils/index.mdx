## Utils

These utilities render little or no chrome of their own. Use them to compose accessible, client-safe, and directional UI.

### [Client Only](./client-only)

Renders children only after the client has mounted.

### [Direction](./direction)

Sets reading direction for a subtree and Radix primitives.

### [Hitbox](./hitbox)

Expands the hit area around a control without changing its look.

### [Pending](./pending)

Marks content as busy and shows a spinner while work is in progress.

### [Portal](./portal)

Renders children into another element.

### [Presence](./presence)

Keeps content mounted while it fades out.

### [Visually Hidden](./visually-hidden)

Hides text visually while keeping it available to assistive tech.

### [Visually Hidden Input](./visually-hidden-input)

A visually hidden input that mirrors a control for form submission.
