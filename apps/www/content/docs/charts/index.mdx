## Charts

Chart families sit in their own section. They share the chart container, tooltip, and legend from the Chart component.

### [Area Chart](./area-chart)

Area chart family — natural, linear, step, stacked, expanded, legend, icons, gradient, axes, and an interactive range.

### [Bar Chart](./bar-chart)

Bar chart family — vertical, horizontal, multiple, stacked, labels, mixed, active, and negative.

### [Line Chart](./line-chart)

Line chart family — natural, linear, step, multiple, dots, custom dots, labels, and an interactive range.

### [Pie Chart](./pie-chart)

Pie chart family — pie, joined slices, labels, legend, donut, active slice, center total, and stacked rings.

### [Radar Chart](./radar-chart)

Radar chart family — default, dots, lines, multiple series, legend, circle grid, filled grid, and icons.

### [Radial Chart](./radial-chart)

Radial chart family — rings, labels, grid, center total, rounded shape, and stacked series.

### [Tooltips](./chart-tooltips)

Tooltip indicators, labels, icons, and value formatting for Aura charts.
