Talk to us

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Marginalia a real product?

No. It is a fictional product that shows how this Aura flavor looks on a real page. Prices and features are invented.

Can I use the flavor without the serif?

You can, but the serif headline is most of the personality. Keep it for display sizes and leave body copy in the sans.

Why are the main buttons black, not clay?

Ink keeps actions calm and readable on ivory. Clay stays small so that when it appears, it means something.

How does dark mode work?

The page turns to warm charcoal and the ink button flips to ivory. The footer stays the darkest band.
