Common questions

Yes — in this demo, Meridian subscribers can keep up to 40 titles on their device, and Zenith subscribers up to 80 titles. Dusk does not include offline saving. Held titles expire after a fictional 30-day window and are linked to the device they were saved on.

Every plan in this demo supports multiple viewer profiles under one account. Each profile keeps its own watch history and recommendations separate. Meridian and Zenith allow up to five profiles; Dusk allows two. All profile data shown here is fictional.

Every rate on this page — monthly, yearly and savings badges — is invented for illustration. Noctilume is a design showcase, not a real service. No purchase is possible here and no payment data is ever collected.

In this fictional demo, all titles carry closed captions and subtitles in multiple languages. Audio description tracks for viewers who are blind or have low vision cover a growing portion of the catalogue. Caption size, colour and background are all adjustable from the player settings — depicted here as example accessibility features.

In this demo scenario you can move from monthly to yearly billing at any time and the lower rate would apply from your next billing date. Switching from yearly back to monthly takes effect once the current annual period ends. All billing logic shown here is illustrative only.

New originals and licensed titles in this fictional service arrive every Thursday evening. A smaller mid-week drop of bonus episodes and short-form content lands on Tuesdays. The schedule shown throughout this page is entirely made up for demonstration purposes.

In this demo scenario, monthly subscribers can pause their plan for up to three months per year. Billing stops during the pause and your profiles, saved titles and watchlist are held until you resume. Yearly subscribers can pause for up to six weeks. Pausing is depicted here as a fictional convenience feature.

Bundles in this demo pair Noctilume with one or both fictional partner services — Dawncast and Vela — under a single login and a combined example rate. The trio bundle adds Ultra 4K HDR, up to five simultaneous screens and an optional live-events add-on. All bundle contents and savings shown are fictional illustrations.
