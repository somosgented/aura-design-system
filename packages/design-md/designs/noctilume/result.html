Yes — Standard and Premium plan subscribers in this demo can save up to 30 or 100 titles respectively for offline playback. Downloads are tied to the device you saved them on and expire after a fictional 30-day window. Starter with Ads does not include offline downloads.

Every plan in this demo supports multiple viewer profiles under one account. Each profile keeps its own watch history and recommendations separate. Standard and Premium plans allow up to five profiles; Starter with Ads allows two. All profile data shown here is fictional.

Every price on this page — monthly rates, yearly totals and savings badges — is invented for demonstration purposes. Noctilume is a design showcase, not a real service. No purchase is possible and no payment information is ever collected here.

In this fictional demo, all titles include closed captions and subtitles in multiple languages. Audio description tracks for viewers who are blind or have low vision are available on a growing portion of the catalogue. Caption size, colour and background can all be adjusted from the player settings — depicted here as example accessibility features.

Yes — in this demo scenario you can move from monthly to yearly billing at any time and the lower rate would apply from the next billing date. Switching from yearly back to monthly takes effect once the current annual period ends. All billing logic shown here is illustrative only.

New originals and licensed titles in this fictional service arrive every Thursday evening. A smaller mid-week drop of bonus episodes and short-form content lands on Tuesdays. The schedule shown throughout this page is entirely made up for demonstration purposes.

In this demo scenario, monthly subscribers can pause their plan for up to three months per year. Billing stops during the pause and your profiles, downloads and watchlist are held until you resume. Yearly subscribers can pause for up to six weeks. Pausing is depicted here as a fictional convenience feature.

Bundles in this demo pair Noctilume with one or both fictional partner services — Dawncast and Vela — under a single login and a combined example price.
