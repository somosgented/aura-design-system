Everything You Need

Lakeside Adventures

Kayaking, paddleboard tours, guided dawn cruises, and sunset boat rides across Lake Lumenvale — activities for every age.

Nature Trails

Over 40 km of waymarked walking and cycling trails through Fernhollow Gardens and beyond, with accessible routes for all.

Copperline Express

When open, our narrow-gauge heritage railway links all six resort areas in a scenic loop — the most charming way to explore.

Lantern Festivals

Seasonal lantern-floating ceremonies on the lake, with live music, storytelling and a sky full of warm light. Bookings required.

Resort Lodging

Forty lakeside lodges from cosy studio cabins to grand waterfront suites, all within easy reach of every resort area.

Meadowlight Events

Live performances, artisan markets and community gatherings in Meadowlight Commons — the social heart of the resort.
