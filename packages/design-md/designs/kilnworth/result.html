Why Kilnworth

Small rooms, clear rules, calm releases.

Each helper gets its own sealed room and a short list of rules. You see what went in, what ran and what came out.

Workflow

Three steps from idea to shipped.

  • Spin up as many sealed rooms as the job needs. Runners join a cell, do their part and leave nothing behind.

    Read the notes

  • A daily line shows how many runs finished cleanly, so slow drifts are easy to spot before they matter.

    Read the notes

  • Each check leaves a short line with a time and a verdict. Scan the list, open any row and see what happened.

Features

Small pieces that fit together.

Seal

Cells that close

Each room has its own files, its own clock and a hard stop, so a helper cannot wander.

Check

Rules you can read

Write a rule in a sentence. Kilnworth turns it into a check that runs before every ship.

Ship

Records that travel

A finished run carries its log, so reviewers can follow the trail without asking around.
