How it works

Every answer shapes the next question.

1

Write it like you talk

Draft questions in plain sentences. Plumline handles numbering, shortcuts and progress.

2

Branch on what matters

Skip what does not apply. A first-timer and a regular never see the same path.

3

Read it as a transcript

Each response arrives as a short conversation, not a row of cells.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Can people still use a mouse or touch?+

Yes. Number keys and Enter are shortcuts, never the only way forward. Every choice is a regular control.

What happens if someone leaves halfway?+

The answers so far are kept as a partial transcript, and the link picks up where they stopped.

Can a question depend on an earlier answer?+

Yes. Branches can skip, reorder or rephrase what comes next.
