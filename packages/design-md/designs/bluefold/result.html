Questions, briefly.

Answers about a fictional product.

Does a helper send anything on its own?

No. In this demo every outgoing message, post or payment waits for your yes.

Which tools can it open?

Only the ones you list in its job. You can remove access at any time.

Can I see what it remembers?

Each note shows the thread it came from and can be deleted.

Is Bluefold a real product?

No. Bluefold is sample content for an Aura design flavor.
