05 / Accordion

Before you begin.

What does a flavor change?

Color seeds, fonts, radius and voice. The 13px grid, components, icons and forms stay Aura.

Where do the colors come from?

theme.css holds generated 12-step accent and gray scales for light and dark. Components read tokens only.

How does my agent apply it?

Give it the DESIGN.md link. It saves DESIGN.theme.md, appends theme.css to globals.css and restyles with Aura components.
