Aura

DESIGN.md flavors

Each flavor adapts Aura to a new look: color seeds, type, radius and voice over the same 13px grid and components. Copy a link and give it to the agent in your Aura project.

Base DESIGN.md
  1. Pick a flavor and press Copy link.
  2. In your Aura project, tell the agent: Apply this design: <link>.
  3. The agent saves it as DESIGN.theme.md, applies its theme.css to globals.css and restyles with Aura components.
