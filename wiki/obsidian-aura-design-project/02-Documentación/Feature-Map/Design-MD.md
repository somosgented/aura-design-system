The narrative design spec that agents and people follow for tokens, type, spacing, motion, and product voice. The registry distributes it to consumer repos.

`packages/design-md` publishes it with a gallery of design flavors. Each flavor is a DESIGN.md that adapts Aura to a new look (color seeds, type, radius, voice) and ships a generated `theme.css`. The gallery shows each flavor as an Aura app preview in light and dark, with a Copy link button. Each flavor has a full-screen detail page with a nav bar and side sheets (Apply, DESIGN.md, Tokens) over its own result page, which keeps the same sections but uses that flavor's composition and signature elements (an Aura app built from the registry `main.css` plus the flavor's `theme.css`), the rendered DESIGN.md and its token scales. It ships as a Cloud Run container (nginx). In an Aura project, the agent gets the link, saves the flavor as `DESIGN.theme.md`, appends `theme.css` to `globals.css`, and restyles with Aura components. The gallery ships ten flavors: Sharpmint, Stillfield, Narc, Bauhaus, Merchantry, Slipstream, Blocksmith, Plumline, Bluefold and Reelnight. Merchantry, Slipstream and Blocksmith interpret the layouts of large commerce, payments and page-builder sites without brands, copy or imagery; Bluefold interprets an AI agent product page from a real desktop screenshot: a white canvas, ink pills, a cream panel and a light desktop-app mockup with colored avatars, without its brand, copy or mascot; Reelnight interprets a public streaming home page measured live: a black screen with a tilted poster wall, one red action, a glowing arc, ranked rows and gray FAQ blocks, with CSS placeholder posters and fictional titles and prices. The old static light and dark previews are gone.

The `generate-design-md` skill creates new flavors from a pasted DESIGN.md, a site, a screenshot or a brief. In this repo it adds the flavor to the gallery with its `theme.css` and showcase; in a consumer app it writes `DESIGN.theme.md` and the generated theme blocks. It ships as `@aura/skill-generate-design-md` and in `@aura/skills`, is linked from the Aura skill, and is documented on the DESIGN.md flavors docs page.
