The narrative design spec that agents and people follow for tokens, type, spacing, motion, and product voice. The registry distributes it to consumer repos.

`packages/design-md` publishes it with a gallery of design flavors. Each flavor is a DESIGN.md that adapts Aura to a new look (color seeds, type, radius, voice) and ships a generated `theme.css`. The gallery shows each flavor as an Aura app preview in light and dark, with a Copy link button. Each flavor has a full-screen detail page with a nav bar and side sheets (Apply, DESIGN.md, Tokens) over its own result page, which keeps the same sections but uses that flavor's composition and signature elements (an Aura app built from the registry `main.css` plus the flavor's `theme.css`), the rendered DESIGN.md and its token scales. It ships as a Cloud Run container (nginx). In an Aura project, the agent gets the link, saves the flavor as `DESIGN.theme.md`, appends `theme.css` to `globals.css`, and restyles with Aura components. The gallery ships nineteen flavors: Sharpmint, Stillfield, Narc, Bauhaus, Merchantry, Slipstream, Blocksmith, Plumline, Bluefold, Nestling, Noctilume, Plinth, Marginalia, Reelnight, Verdantline, Hushtone, Lumenvale, Reedwave and Tillwright.

Noctilume interprets the layout of a large evening streaming home without brands, copy or imagery.

Merchantry, Slipstream and Blocksmith interpret the layouts of large commerce, payments and page-builder sites without brands, copy or imagery; Bluefold interprets an AI agent product page from a real desktop screenshot: a white canvas, ink pills, a cream panel and a light desktop-app mockup with colored avatars, without its brand, copy or mascot; Nestling interprets a playful wallet home page from real 1280 and 360 captures: centered display headlines flanked by flat bobbing critters, ink and cream pills and warm off-white panels, with original illustrations, fictional demo data and no wallet connection or secret fields; Plinth interprets a workspace SaaS marketing home page from one-time 1280 and 360 reference captures: oversized Inter headlines, ink and soft-gray pill CTAs, band sections and bento product vignettes, with fictional Plinth copy and original UI drawings and no source logos or trademarks; Marginalia interprets an AI assistant product page: warm ivory paper, near-black ink buttons, a bookish serif and one clay margin rule, without brands, copy or imagery; Reelnight interprets a streaming home page: black screen, tilted poster wall, one action red, luminous hero arc, Top 10 row and gray FAQ blocks, with fictional titles and no brands, copy or imagery; Verdantline interprets a public developer-platform marketing home (observed 2026-11-08): IBM Plex Sans and Mono, 6px controls, mint-green hero CTAs, overlapping avatar row, code vignettes and a footer with a mint top rule, with fictional Verdantline copy and no proprietary marks from the reference site; Hushtone is an original sound-wellness flavor: a night-black canvas with thin white line drawings, glowing ink controls, a rayed device field, stats with footnotes and a newsletter footer, with a daylight light mode that keeps night islands and a pause control for ambient motion; Lumenvale is an original lakeside lantern park flavor featuring a sticky two-row royal-blue header with mega-menu and slim utility-row search pill, an animated CSS night-scene hero (sky gradient, hill silhouettes, lake reflection band, lantern particles) with a circular pause control and sheet-over-hero page body, a booking strip floating over the hero at desktop, an area hours disclosure, offer and experience cards, alternating planning rows, a seasonal mosaic, and a full-width legal footer — delivered in a light mode and an adapted dark mode that reinterprets the deep-blue lantern-warm palette as a night-time lakeside scene; Reedwave is an original messaging flavor: a cream canvas, near-black green ink, reed-green pills, chat bubbles over drawn device mockups, a night privacy band with a pausable text animation and a mint news carousel, with fictional copy, a demo sign-up panel that sends nothing and no external imagery. Tillwright is an original commerce-platform flavor: ink-rectangle buttons with 2 px radius, periwinkle second-line headlines, a two-tier header with a full-width mega menu, lavender panels, a night-navy rounded band, monospaced uppercase eyebrows, fictional copy and figures, a demo form that sends nothing, and no external imagery. The old static light and dark previews are gone.

The `generate-design-md` skill creates new flavors from a pasted DESIGN.md, a site, a screenshot or a brief. In this repo it adds the flavor to the gallery with its `theme.css` and showcase; in a consumer app it writes `DESIGN.theme.md` and the generated theme blocks. It ships as `@aura/skill-generate-design-md` and in `@aura/skills`, is linked from the Aura skill, and is documented on the DESIGN.md flavors docs page.
