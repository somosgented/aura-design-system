Avatar shows a person image, and when that image is missing it shows initials or an icon inside a gray circle. The fallback uses the high-contrast text step so those initials and icons stay readable on the circle in light and dark themes. The component lives in the registry and on the homepage Themes preview and the Avatar docs page.
